## Domain 5

See [items 19–24](domain-5-selective-reporting.md).

| Condition | Domain judgment |
|---|---|
| 19=NI/N | Some concerns |
| 21=N/NI | Some concerns |
| 22=Y | Low risk |
| 22=NI | Some concerns |
| 22=N | High risk |
| 22=NI and 24=N | High risk |
| 22=Y and 24=N | High risk |

The last two rows override the ordinary item-22 result when an unjustified departure from the plan makes item 24=`N`. The domain prose says item 24 otherwise does not enter the algorithm.

## Overall score

| Domain profile | Overall judgment |
|---|---|
| All five domains low risk | Low risk |
| At least one high-risk domain | High risk |
| At least three domains with some concerns | High risk |
| One or two domains with some concerns and no high-risk domain | Some concerns |

The overall rule is therefore not simply the worst label: three or more “some concerns” domains escalate the trial to high risk.
