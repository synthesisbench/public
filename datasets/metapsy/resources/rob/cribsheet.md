# MetaPsy risk-of-bias decision cribsheet

Use this when deciding a MetaPsy signalling item or domain for one trial result. It is a decision aid, not a substitute for reading every staged report. The fuller local rules are in the [MetaPsy handbook index](handbook/README.md) and [domain algorithms](handbook/algorithms.md).

## Fix the assessment target first

Write down the target before judging:

- study and randomized comparison;
- outcome domain, instrument or assessor, time point, and exact numerical result;
- effect of **assignment** to intervention (the MetaPsy target), not adherence to intervention.

Domains 1 and much of 2 may reuse study-level facts, but the judgment still belongs to this result. Domains 2–5 can change with the outcome, time point, instrument, analysis, or reporting choice. Never transfer a rating merely because another result came from the same trial.

If the task asks only for domain labels, use the items below as internal decision support and emit only the requested lowercase labels: `low`, `unclear`, or `high`. The source label **some concerns** maps to task label `unclear`.

## Evidence precedence and response vocabulary

### Reconcile all staged sources

1. Link companion reports by trial; do not treat each paper as a separate study.
2. For **what was planned and when**, use the earliest timestamped registry version, protocol, or SAP that predates unmasked outcome data. A later edited registry cannot establish prospective specification.
3. For **what actually happened or was analyzed**, use the final report and results supplements. A protocol proves intent, not execution, and cannot override a reported departure.
4. Merge compatible details across sources. Record both sides of a conflict. If actual-conduct sources conflict and the conflict changes the answer, use `NI` unless one source establishes a concrete failure, in which case `No/PN` may be warranted. Do not cherry-pick the more favorable account.
5. Use only locally staged documents. A cited but unavailable registry or plan is not accessible evidence.

### Categorical fields

- `Yes/PY`: the evidence supports yes, including a justified “probably yes.”
- `No/PN`: the evidence supports no, including a justified “probably no.”
- `NI`: reporting is insufficient and neither direction is reasonably supported. Do not use it merely because the exact words are absent when the method is otherwise clear.
- `N/A`: only for a question skipped by an explicit branch. It is not a synonym for `NI`.

Algorithm tables below abbreviate `Yes/PY` as `Y`, `No/PN` as `N`, and `NI` as `I`.

Answer items independently except for branch skips. In each domain note (`d1_notes` through `d5_notes`), identify the target result, source and page/section, a brief quotation or exact counts, any calculation, and why the evidence maps to the response. Explain every expert override.

| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1 · `d1_1` — Was a random method used to generate the allocation sequence?**              | Randomization methods in the report/protocol: computer-generated numbers, random-number table, dice, coin toss, or another process with chance.                | `Yes/PY` for a chance-based sequence; `No/PN` for alternation, dates, record numbers, clinician choice, or another deterministic/predictable rule; `NI` when only “randomized” is stated and no reasonable inference is possible; `N/A` never. | Do not infer sequence generation from balanced groups. Record the method and source; if minimization or another adaptive method has no clear random element, seek expert adjudication.                                                                                                                                 |
| **2 · `d1_2` — Was allocation concealed from people enrolling and assigning participants?** | Who held the sequence and how the next assignment was released: independent central/remote allocation or properly sealed opaque envelopes support concealment. | `Yes/PY` when recruiters could not predict or influence the next assignment; `No/PN` for an open list, unsealed/translucent envelopes, or a predictable sequence available to recruiters; `NI` when concealment is not described; `N/A` never. | Concealment is **before assignment**, not participant/provider masking afterward. Fixed blocks can be predictable when size and prior assignments are known. Record mechanism, sequence custodian, and recruiter access.                                                                                               |
| **3 · `d1_3` — Are randomized group sizes balanced?**                                       | Planned allocation ratio and randomized counts, before attrition. Compare the observed arm proportion with the planned proportion using a 99% CI.              | `Yes/PY` when the expected proportion lies inside the 99% CI; `No/PN` when the CI excludes it; `NI` when counts or planned ratio are unavailable; `N/A` never.                                                                                 | “Balanced” means compatible with the planned ratio, not exactly equal and not equal completer counts. Record ratio, randomized counts, proportion, CI, and tool used. Multi-arm calculations not resolved by the local source require the live RoB Assistant or expert review.                                         |
| **4 · `d1_4` — Is baseline clinical severity balanced?**                                    | Arm-level baseline severity means, SDs, and sample sizes for the principal prognostic measure; calculate baseline SMD and 99% CI.                              | `No/PN` when **both** SMD is greater than 0.2 and the 99% CI lower limit is greater than 0; `Yes/PY` when the stated imbalance signal is absent; `NI` when required baseline data are unavailable; `N/A` never.                                | Do not treat chance imbalance as randomization failure. For multiple severity tests, consider Bonferroni adjustment. Record measure, arm statistics, SMD/CI, and clinically important disorder-specific prognostic imbalances. Use the live tool/expert for orientation or clinically important cases the rule misses. |

## Domain 2 — Deviations from intended interventions

Full rules: [items 5–9](handbook/domain-2-deviations.md) · [algorithm](handbook/algorithms.md#domain-2)

| Item                                                                                                                    | Look for                                                                                                                                                          | Choose                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Trap; record                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **5 · `d2_5` — Were participants masked to intervention?**                                                              | Intervention descriptions, consent framing, credibility/expectancy procedures, and explicit participant masking.                                                  | `Yes/PY` only when participants plausibly could not distinguish assignment; `No/PN` when they knew or could readily infer it, as in most psychotherapy trials; `NI` when assignment awareness cannot be inferred; `N/A` never.                                                                  | Calling a trial “single/double blind” does not identify who was masked. Two equally presented active therapies or some non-inferiority designs may qualify only after close examination. Record who was masked and how.                                                                                                              |
| **6 · `d2_6` — Were carers and intervention deliverers masked?**                                                        | Who delivered therapy/care and whether they knew each participant's assignment.                                                                                   | `Yes/PY` when carers/deliverers were credibly unaware; `No/PN` when they knew, as is usual for therapists delivering a named psychotherapy; `NI` when roles or awareness are unclear; `N/A` never.                                                                                              | Outcome-assessor masking belongs to item 18. Record each relevant role rather than relying on a global blinding label.                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **7 · `d2_7` — If 5 or 6 is N/I, did interventions remain close to plan without important result-changing deviations?** | Protocol-defined care, treatment received, contamination, co-interventions, monitoring, switching, and reasons for departures, by arm and for the target outcome. | `Yes/PY` when no deviation simultaneously arose from the trial context/lack of masking, could affect this outcome, and was meaningfully unbalanced; `No/PN` when all three features are present; `NI` when conduct or arm balance is insufficiently reported; `N/A` only when both 5 and 6=Y.   | A deviation merely occurring is insufficient. Ordinary real-world nonadherence does not by itself bias the assignment effect; balanced or outcome-irrelevant deviations are also less concerning. A 20 percentage-point arm difference is a rough signal, not a complete rule. Record type, cause, outcome relevance, and arm rates. |
| **8 · `d2_8` — Was the analysis appropriate for the effect of assignment?**                                             | Analysis population, randomized denominators, exclusions, crossover handling, and which arm participants were analyzed in.                                        | `Yes/PY` when participants are analyzed as randomized; an mITT analysis excluding only missing endpoints still qualifies here; `No/PN` for as-treated, naive per-protocol, or exclusions based on treatment receipt/adherence; `NI` when the analysis set cannot be reconstructed; `N/A` never. | Do not trust an “ITT” label without checking membership. Missing endpoints are judged in domain 3, not as an adherence exclusion here. Record analysis set, exclusions/reassignments, reasons, and denominators.                                                                                                                     |
| **9 · `d2_9` — If 8 is N/I, is the inappropriate analysis unlikely to affect the result?**                              | Number randomized versus number excluded for receipt/adherence or analyzed in the wrong arm.                                                                      | `Yes/PY` when affected participants are **below 5%**; `No/PN` when **above 5%**; `NI` when the fraction cannot be calculated; `N/A` when 8=Y. Exactly 5% is not resolved by the source: use the live tool or expert.                                                                            | Do not count ordinary missing outcome questionnaires here. Record numerator, denominator, percentage, and exclusion/reassignment reason.                                                                                                                                                                                             |

_Micro-example:_ if unmasked wait-list participants seek the experimental psychotherapy outside the trial and this occurs materially more often than in the intervention arm, item 7 can be `No/PN`. Stopping a treatment for a reason that would arise in routine care is not enough by itself.

| Responses                 | Task judgment |
| ------------------------- | ------------- |
| 5=Y and 6=Y               | `low`         |
| 7=Y/I and 8=Y             | `low`         |
| 7=Y/I, 8=N/I, and 9=Y     | `unclear`     |
| 7=Y/I, 8=N/I, and 9=N/I   | `high`        |
| 7=N, regardless of 8 or 9 | `high`        |

## Domain 3 — Missing outcome data

Full rules: [items 10–14](handbook/domain-3-missing-data.md) · [algorithm](handbook/algorithms.md#domain-3)

| Item                                                                                                  | Look for                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Choose                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Trap; record                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **10 · `d3_10` — Were outcome data obtained from nearly all randomized participants?**                | For the target instrument/time point, randomized and **observed endpoint** counts by arm; for dichotomous outcomes, also event counts.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Continuous: `Yes/PY` when observed availability is **above 95% in both arms**; `No/PN` when it is not (exactly 95% does not meet “above”); `NI` when counts are unavailable. Dichotomous: use `Yes/PY` only when missing participants are few relative to observed events such that material bias is implausible, `No/PN` when missingness could materially alter the result, and `NI` when this cannot be judged. `N/A` never. Close dichotomous cases need the live tool/expert. | LOCF or other imputed values are not observed endpoints. Do not use a study-wide follow-up rate for the wrong result. Record randomized/observed/event counts and arm percentages.                                                                                               |
| **11 · `d3_11` — If 10 is N/I, was missingness handled appropriately?**                               | Model specification and sensitivity analyses. Accepted routes include MMRM/mixed or growth models with at least two post-baseline measurements; multiple imputation that represents uncertainty, uses important auxiliary/intermediate outcomes, imputes by randomized arm, and uses an appropriate controlled/reference-based method; or sensitivity analyses spanning plausible mechanisms and supporting the result. | `Yes/PY` when an accepted analysis protects this result; `No/PN` for LOCF, unprotected complete-case analysis, or another clearly inadequate method; `NI` when reporting is too thin to assess; `N/A` when 10=Y.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | A method name alone is insufficient, and an “ITT” label does not solve missingness. Record model/imputation details, auxiliary variables, sensitivity range, and whether conclusions changed.                                                                                    |
| **12 · `d3_12` — If 11 is N/I, did at least 40 participants per arm provide outcome data?**           | Reported-data count for the target result in every randomized arm.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | `Yes/PY` when every arm has **at least 40** observed participants; `No/PN` when any arm has fewer than 40; `NI` when counts are missing; `N/A` when the branch is not reached.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Use outcome providers, not randomized sample size. Record per-arm counts.                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **13 · `d3_13` — If 11 is N/I, did at least 70% in each arm complete post-treatment assessment?**     | Randomized and post-treatment completer counts by arm.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | `Yes/PY` when **at least 70% in every arm** completed; `No/PN` when any arm is below 70%; `NI` when the rate cannot be calculated; `N/A` when the branch is not reached.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | The source explicitly names post-treatment. Do not silently transplant this threshold to another time point; if the target is not post-treatment and applicability matters, use the live tool/expert. Record counts, denominators, and percentages by arm.                       |
| **14 · `d3_14` — If 11 is N/I, is there indirect evidence that missingness is unrelated to outcome?** | Reasons for missingness, arm-specific dropout proportions, and whether reasons differ by arm or relate to severity/unobserved outcome.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | `Yes/PY` only when reporting covers all three and shows no outcome-related reason, no substantial arm imbalance, and no concerning reason imbalance; `No/PN` if any warning is present; `NI` when evidence is insufficient; `N/A` when the branch is not reached.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | A funding stop is plausibly external; lack of efficacy or worsening is outcome-related. Below 20 percentage points is the source's rough arm-difference rule; exactly 20 is unresolved. Balanced dropout alone cannot prove independence. Record arm rates and reasons verbatim. |

## Domain 4 — Measurement of the outcome

Full rules: [items 15–18](handbook/domain-4-outcome-measurement.md) · [algorithm](handbook/algorithms.md#domain-4)

| Item                                                       | Look for                                                                                                                                                             | Choose                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Trap; record                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **15 · `d4_15` — Was the measurement method appropriate?** | Evidence that the target instrument validly and reliably measures the target outcome, and that administration/ascertainment procedures were comparable between arms. | `Yes/PY` when both appropriateness and comparability are supported; `No/PN` when the instrument is inappropriate or procedures differ materially; `NI` when either cannot be assessed; `N/A` never.                                                                                                                                 | A familiar scale is not automatically valid for every population/outcome, and equal instrument names do not prove equal ascertainment. Record instrument, assessor, timing, mode, validity cue, and arm procedures. Item 15=N is not exhaustively mapped for every source algorithm path; adjudicate rather than extrapolate.                                 |
| **16 · `d4_16` — Were self-report measures used?**         | Who supplied and recorded the target rating, where it was completed, and whether therapists/researchers could see responses.                                         | `Yes/PY` for a self-report result when there is no evidence of under-reporting, especially when responses were shielded from therapists; `No/PN` when no self-report result was used **or** when under-reporting is plausible, such as completion in front of a therapist; `NI` when rating source/context is unclear; `N/A` never. | The wording is descriptive, but the handbook also directs `No/PN` for plausible under-reporting. If that produces a response combination absent from the algorithm, use the live tool/expert. A participant remains the source of a participant-reported outcome even if an interviewer enters answers. Record respondent and privacy safeguards or pressure. |
| **17 · `d4_17` — Were assessor-rated instruments used?**   | Structured interviews or behavioral observations scored by a therapist, independent evaluator, or research staff member for the target result.                       | `Yes/PY` when such an assessor rating contributes; `No/PN` when it does not; `NI` when rating provenance is unclear; `N/A` never.                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Do not confuse data-entry assistance for self-report with assessor judgment. If the target combines self- and assessor-rated results, items 16 and 17 can both be Y. Record instrument and assessor role.                                                                                                                                                     |
| **18 · `d4_18` — If 17=Y, were outcome assessors masked?** | An explicit statement identifying target-result assessors as unaware of allocation, plus any unmasking information.                                                  | `Yes/PY` only when masking is clearly reported; `No/PN` when assessors were aware **or masking is not reported**; `NI` for an unresolved conflict/ambiguity despite relevant reporting; `N/A` when 17 is not Y.                                                                                                                     | “Double blind,” participant masking, or provider masking does not establish assessor masking. Record who was masked, how, and any evidence masking failed.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

For both self- and assessor-rated measurements in one outcome domain, follow the review protocol: either rate each numerical result separately or use the protocol-defined aggregate. MetaPsy's aggregate option is conservative—any high-risk component makes the aggregate high risk. Do not choose the strategy after seeing ratings.

Apply the review protocol's eligible-outcome rule. A protocol that requires every instrument has a different completeness test from a pre-specified instrument hierarchy.

| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **19 · `d5_19` — Is a registration, protocol, or SAP available?**                           | A locally accessible registry record/version history, protocol, or SAP linked to the trial.                                                                                                                                 | `Yes/PY` when a relevant document is accessible; `No/PN` only when evidence supports that none exists; `NI` when its existence/content is unknown or a cited document cannot be accessed; `N/A` never. Record version/date and all sources used.                                                                                                                                       |
| **21 · `d5_21` — If 19=Y, were outcome scale and time point specified prospectively?**      | Earliest registry history/protocol: recruitment start, posting/acceptance date, later amendments, and the target outcome's scale and time point.                                                                            | `Yes/PY` when scale and time point were specified before unmasked outcome data; `No/PN` when evidence shows they were added/changed after that point; `NI` for retrospective registration, missing scale/time, or unresolved timing; `N/A` when 19 is not Y.          | The source mentions both “before data collection” and a one-month window around enrollment start without fully reconciling them. Do not invent a boundary decision; use the live tool/expert for cases dependent on that window. Record dates, earliest wording, and changes. |
| **22 · `d5_22` — If 21=Y, are all outcomes required by the meta-analysis fully available?** | Review protocol's eligible instrument rule; all papers/supplements and other staged routes for usable data at the target time point.                                                                                        | `Yes/PY` when every result required by the review rule is usable; `No/PN` when a required result is missing/incomplete or the publication adds an unregistered outcome; `NI` when completeness cannot be established; `N/A` when 21 is not Y.                         | Judge against the review protocol, not the paper's preferred outcome. Data supplied through another documented route can count. Record the eligible set, located set, missing/additional results, and why the target was selected.                                            |
| **23 · `d5_23` — If 19=Y, was an analysis plan pre-specified?**                             | Earliest plan's timing and details: analysis population, metric, covariates, missing-data handling, model, and contrasts. Published plan/protocol timing or acceptance should precede enrollment under the source guidance. | `Yes/PY` when a genuine plan predates unblinded outcome information; `No/PN` when no plan exists or evidence shows it was created later; `NI` when content, version, or timing is unclear; `N/A` when 19 is not Y.                                                    | A prospectively named outcome is not necessarily a pre-specified analysis. Record plan version/date and the target-result analysis specification.                                                                                                                             |
| **24 · `d5_24` — If 23=Y, was the result analyzed according to plan?**                      | Side-by-side comparison of planned and reported analysis, disclosed amendments, timing, rationale, and likely effect on this result.                                                                                        | `Yes/PY` when the plan was followed or a disclosed, well-justified departure does not create material selection concern; `No/PN` for an unjustified result-dependent departure; `NI` when comparison is impossible; `N/A` when 23 is not Y.                           | Do not penalize every deviation mechanically, but do not accept an explanation written after results without scrutiny. ## Overall judgment and escalation

Use the MetaPsy overall rule only after all five domain judgments are resolved:

| Domain profile                           | Overall task judgment |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| All five `low`                           | `low`                 |
| Any `high`                               | `high`                |
| At least three `unclear` and none `high` | `high`                |
| One or two `unclear` and none `high`     | `unclear`             |

Algorithms propose judgments; they do not replace a material-bias judgment. Record a reason for any override. Escalate to the live RoB Assistant or an expert when a published algorithm row is missing, a stated boundary is unresolved (notably exactly 5% or the registration window), a rough 20-point rule is borderline, a non-parallel/cluster/crossover design needs adaptation, calculations/orientation are uncertain, or clinically important evidence is not represented by the items. Never invent a mapping.
